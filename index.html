Tune your style

LLM prompt — hand this to any AI to reproduce the style

Paste it into your AI tool with the instruction “Build my project in exactly this visual style”, and attach the PNG if the model accepts images. The full spec front-loads a plain-English summary, do/don't rules, and paste-ready CSS tokens so even a small model lands on the right look; the later sections give a capable model the exact craft CSS and structure contract. The .css download also works on its own: add class="style-scope" to <body> and link it.
